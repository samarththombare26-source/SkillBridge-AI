# Problem Statement

Students face several challenges while preparing for their careers.

Problems include:

- No personalized career guidance
- Difficulty finding internships
- Skill mismatch with industry
- Poor resume quality
- Lack of mentor support
- Multiple websites for different services
- No centralized career platform

SkillBridgeAI solves these problems by combining career guidance, AI analysis, resume building, internship management, and mentor support into one platform.
